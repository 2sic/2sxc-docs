# Contribute to 2sxc / EAV Standard Apps

[!include[""](../code/_contributors-only.md)]

These are internal guidelines for contributing Apps to the 2sxc / EAV standard App collection.

---

## Location

1. All our standard Apps are developed in our `apps-dev` DNN installation.
1. All our standard Apps are published in this git org: <https://github.com/2sxc-apps>  
    Note: historically some are still in <https://github.com/2sxc>, but we're working on moving them.

## Central Catalog with Quality Check Features

All our apps, themes, extensions etc. on the `apps-dev` DNN must be registered
in the central catalog on the home page.

This catalog also has special features to check the quality of the apps.

➡️ See [](xref:Abyss.Contribute.Apps.Quality)
